PROFESSIONAL WRESTLING SCHOOL

YOU DON'T LEARN THIS FROM THE COUCH.

Professional wrestling takes more than watching matches and memorizing moves. It takes repetition, conditioning, control, timing and the willingness to keep learning.

The 209 Dragon's Den welcomes beginners taking their first bump and experienced wrestlers looking for a place to sharpen their craft.

WHAT YOU'LL LEARN

BUILD THE FOUNDATION.

Wrestling starts with fundamentals. Everything else gets built on top of them.

01

IN-RING FUNDAMENTALS

Learn movement, holds, strikes, slams, positioning and the mechanics that make professional wrestling work.

02

SAFETY & CONTROL

Learn how to protect yourself, protect your opponent and perform with control.

03

CONDITIONING & STRENGTH

Build the endurance, strength and athletic foundation needed to perform inside the ring.

04

CHARACTER DEVELOPMENT

Learn how to develop your persona, communicate your character and connect with a live wrestling audience.

TWO DISCIPLINES

EXPAND YOUR TOOLBOX.

AMERICAN STYLE

PRO WRESTLING

Fundamentals, psychology, match structure, character work and traditional professional wrestling technique.

LUCHA LIBRE

MOVE DIFFERENT.

Learn another wrestling language through Lucha Libre movement, technique, timing and style.

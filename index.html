THIS IS PROFESSIONAL WRESTLING

YOU DON'T LEARN THIS FROM THE COUCH.

The 209 Dragon's Den is a professional wrestling training academy in Lodi, California built for people who want to experience the craft for themselves.

From your first bump to building matches and connecting with a live crowd, training at the Den develops the complete performer.

WHAT YOU'LL LEARN

BUILD THE COMPLETE WRESTLER.
